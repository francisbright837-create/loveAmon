SHADOW DIMENSION

Chapter 1: The World Beyond the Gate

Ren, a young warrior, discovers an ancient portal hidden beneath the ruins of his hometown. A mysterious voice calls to him from beyond it. When he touches it, the ground collapses and he is dragged into another dimension: the Shadow Dimension.

It was once a beautiful world of forests, rivers, villages and powerful warriors, until a terrible war between humans and demons destroyed everything. Now abandoned buildings rise from the earth, burned-out cars and broken military vehicles lie across ruined roads, and demons hunt anyone who enters.

Ren carries a mysterious power: the Shadow Core. Every demon he defeats feeds it, and his strength grows. But the deeper he goes, the stronger the demons become, and something powerful waits at the end of every realm.

Chapter 2: The Five Realms

1. The Fallen City. Weak demons, ruined buildings, the first shrine. Boss: the Gatekeeper Demon.

2. The Cursed Forest. Giant trees, poisonous plants and fast demons. Boss: the Forest Guardian.

3. The Drowned Kingdom. Flooded villages, deep rivers and aquatic demons.

4. The Battlefield of Souls. Destroyed cars, tanks and military bases. Elite demons hunt you.

5. The Demon King’s Domain. A fortress of dark energy. Defeat the demon generals, then face the Demon King.

Chapter 3: The Truth of the Shadow Core

Defeat the boss of each realm to uncover why the portal chose Ren, and what the Shadow Core really is.
